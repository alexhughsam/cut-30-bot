# Cut30 Playbook — Oren John method, distilled

**Provenance warning (read every session):** Cut30 is a paid bootcamp; the full method is
paywalled. This playbook was built 2026-07-06 from public sources only. The research
environment blocked full-page fetches (proxy 403s on every direct fetch), so *all*
citations below were verified via search-index snippets and page titles — verbatim where
the snippet reproduced Oren's own words (tweets, captions, newsletter excerpts), weaker
where the search layer summarized. Each entry carries a confidence level. A fresh
reviewer has traced every `[DOCUMENTED]` claim; anything it couldn't trace was
downgraded. **The user's own bootcamp notes are gospel and override everything here.**

**Tags:**
- `[DOCUMENTED]` — Oren (or Cut30's own material) actually said it; source cited.
  Sub-levels: *verbatim* (his exact words in the snippet), *paraphrase* (close summary of
  his own page/post), *secondhand* (a third party reporting what he said/does).
- `[MY READ]` — my extension or synthesis. Not Oren's words. Never present these to the
  user as Cut30 doctrine.

## 1. Hooks

- `[DOCUMENTED — verbatim]` **Every hook has a visual AND an audio component; the hook
  must matter to the audience.** "Improving our hooks training - reinforcing that every
  hook has a visual and audio component - hammering in that the hook needs to matter to
  audience…" — https://x.com/orenmeetsworld/status/1808175116142236153
- `[DOCUMENTED — verbatim, tail truncated]` **The "ending hook": a second hook at the end
  of the video** — same tweet: "doubling down on 'ending hook' concept where you add a
  second…" (search index summarizes the truncated tail as: to get people to rewatch).
- `[DOCUMENTED — paraphrase]` **A provocative hook doesn't hurt you if what follows is
  immediately measured, intelligent, and credible** — the hook is setup, the credibility
  is the payoff. His example: opening a Coachella video with "isn't this a complete
  waste?" then immediately pivoting to the economics of attention. — Viral Content
  Masterclass (Motion/Goldcast),
  https://motioncreativeanalytics.ondemand.goldcast.io/on-demand/d894c282-4e78-4956-aa27-d4b3591219bc
- `[DOCUMENTED — verbatim]` **Recognition is becoming a second stopping force.** "The
  hook still reigns supreme—but in an overwhelming feed, people increasingly stop because
  they recognize you, not just because you grabbed attention. Once you achieve
  recognition, the next job is comfort. You build that through a styled environment or
  'set'… or through a recognizable visual system." — "The State of Content 2026,"
  https://www.hyperstudios.us/p/the-state-of-content-2026
- `[MY READ]` Operational hook test derived from the above: grade a hook on three axes —
  (1) visual component (what's in the first frame), (2) audio/line component (first words),
  (3) audience relevance (why *this* viewer cares). A hook that only scores on the line is
  half a hook.
- **NOT FOUND:** No citable Oren statement on "first 3 seconds"-style second-counts.
  Don't quote one as his.

## 2. Retention & rewatch

- `[DOCUMENTED — verbatim]` **Value wrapped in story, with visuals, is what retains.**
  "2025 was about story. The storyteller thrived… Value alone slowed, value wrapped in a
  good story, with accompanying visuals thrived." — "The State of Content 2026,"
  https://www.hyperstudios.us/p/the-state-of-content-2026
- `[DOCUMENTED — verbatim]` **Formula for service businesses:** "Exciting scenarios, real
  world tie ins, and true education. If you don't want to be on camera, do it in carousels
  (but will go much slower)." — https://www.tiktok.com/@orenmeetsworld/video/7503608361716321566
- `[DOCUMENTED — paraphrase]` **The hottest videos are 2–3 minutes long and come from
  subject-matter experts; generic content reads as noise.** Cut through by talking about
  ideas, backing them visually, having a unique POV, making things friends would share. —
  "The noise economy," https://www.hyperstudios.us/p/the-noise-economy-and-how-to-rise
- `[MY READ]` The rewatch mechanics this coach will engineer (Oren names the *goal* —
  ending hook, rewatchability — publicly, but not the full toolkit): open loop closed in
  the last line, a detail that means something different on second viewing, an ending
  hook that recontextualizes the opening, density high enough that a viewer misses
  something the first time.
- **NOT FOUND:** No citable Oren material on reading retention graphs or loop-editing
  tricks. Autopsy craft below is `[MY READ]` unless tagged otherwise.

## 3. Scripting & production workflow

- `[DOCUMENTED — verbatim]` **Pipeline: content category → idea → bullet points → script
  → record; then repurpose.** "I start with an idea in a specific content category, which
  I then turn into bullet points and eventually a script to record." —
  https://www.tiktok.com/@orenmeetsworld/video/7272380214351662382 (Notion-sponsored,
  disclosed)
- `[DOCUMENTED — verbatim]` **Reps are the teacher.** "i made bad 50 bad short form
  videos it took two months to get one to 5k" (his framing: three weeks doing something
  teaches you nothing). — https://x.com/orenmeetsworld/status/1938392071527887030
- `[DOCUMENTED — secondhand]` One video per day for ~24 months (from Feb 2023), built
  while working full-time, 0→400k followers. —
  https://startupspells.com/p/oren-john-instagram-playbook-bold-fonts-vibrant-shades-luxury-aesthetic
- `[DOCUMENTED — paraphrase]` Cut30 itself teaches **copy-work** — studying and
  rewriting winning scripts — as the week-1 scripting method. — https://course.cut30.co/sales-page
- `[MY READ]` Coaching implication: when the user brings a messy idea, run Oren's public
  pipeline in miniature — force the one core message, bullet the beats, then script — and
  use the swipe file as the copy-work corpus.

## 4. Visuals, first frame, editing

- `[DOCUMENTED — verbatim]` **Visible effort in visuals builds trust.** "Not
  overproduction. Not perfection. Effort. Animations that guide attention. Thoughtful
  transitions. Format switching that creates rhythm. Graphics that feel designed, not
  generated." — "The State of Content 2026," https://www.hyperstudios.us/p/the-state-of-content-2026
- `[DOCUMENTED — paraphrase]` **Set/staging is a virality lever** — a "completely normal"
  podcast becomes shareable through visual staging (therapist's-couch set, overhead
  camera, wide shots). — https://www.tiktok.com/@orenmeetsworld/video/7560738567203327263
- `[DOCUMENTED — secondhand]` Face in thumbnails/covers for fast trust; bold colors,
  clean fonts, sharp keyword headlines. — startupspells.com (analysis of his visible
  practice, not his words)
- `[DOCUMENTED — paraphrase]` **"Yap" formats** — low-lift talking formats carried by a
  visual device: notebook yaps, object yaps, car yaps, walking yaps, graphic yaps. —
  "How to yap…", https://www.hyperstudios.us/p/how-to-yap-advanced-influence-and
- `[DOCUMENTED — paraphrase]` **Brands-as-TV:** structure content like a show — people
  are characters, locations are sets, announcements/holidays are plot; episodes and arcs
  sustain attention. — https://www.linkedin.com/posts/orenj_marketingstrategy-contentstrategy-brandcontent-activity-7222625939040477187-Xjby

## 5. Performance analysis & iteration

- `[DOCUMENTED — paraphrase]` **Content pillars system:** run 3–5 pillars at a time,
  5–10 pieces per pillar per month, evaluate monthly; when a pillar falls off, *elevate*
  it before killing it. — "How to Use Content Pillars,"
  https://www.hyperstudios.us/p/how-to-use-content-pillars
- `[DOCUMENTED — secondhand, weak]` He tracks posts, saves, shares, views, and
  month-over-month change in a spreadsheet, weighting "social intent" metrics
  (saves/shares); data as "ammunition to be in charge of strategy." Primary source
  unpinned — treat as a lead, not doctrine.
- `[DOCUMENTED — secondhand, weak]` "No one in the network has gotten meaningful results
  without being on camera for some of their content." — attributed via the Blueprint
  podcast episode cluster; primary source unverified.
- `[DOCUMENTED — paraphrase]` **Organic before paid:** "start with organic… build that
  muscle. And then… performance marketing is meant to amplify what you're already doing."
  — Cyber Patterns interview, https://www.cyberpatterns.xyz/p/oren
- `[DOCUMENTED — secondhand]` Good organic hooks get harvested for paid ads; test hooks,
  angles, personas systematically. — Open Residency podcast (summary only)
- `[MY READ]` Autopsy method for this coach (Oren publishes *what* he tracks, not a
  retention-graph procedure): read the numbers against the user's own baseline, find the
  earliest big divergence (hook-rate/first-seconds drop vs. mid-video drop vs.
  completion-without-shares), name ONE lever pinned to evidence, prescribe ONE change to
  test next video. Weight saves/shares as intent signals, consistent with his documented
  emphasis.

## 6. Coaching stance (all `[MY READ]`, grounded in the above)

- Every script review grades: first-frame visual + first-line audio (per §1), audience
  relevance, one core message, density (every word does stop-the-scroll or rewatch work),
  and a nameable rewatch reason (ending hook per §1).
- Every autopsy: baseline first, evidence-pinned single lever, one change to test,
  and a check on whether last video's prescribed change was tested and what it did.
- Reps compound: each video feeds the swipe file (SWIPE_FILE.md); scripts start from
  what already worked for *this* user, not from templates.
